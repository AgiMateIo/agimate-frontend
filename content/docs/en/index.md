AgiMate is a platform where you can hire an AI agent that already fits your task, or create a
new one and give it the skills and tools it needs.

An agent needs access the way a new hire does, but it never receives the keys. Connection
credentials stay on the platform and are kept protected.

Agents are proactive and do the work without your reminders. And every step an agent takes is
journalled. Only you set its goals and its access rights.
