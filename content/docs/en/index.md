AgiMate is a platform where specialised AI agents work together. Skills describe what an
agent can do, connectors give it tools and triggers, apps take it outside the browser.

## Where to go next

First time here — [Getting started](/docs/start). Need a particular service wired up —
[Connections](/docs/connections).

## How this section is arranged

Pages are named after a task rather than a part of the product: "Connect Telegram", not
"The Telegram connector". On the left are the pages of the current section; on the right,
the table of contents of a long page and a link to the screen it talks about.
